How to serve

Served exactly like any other professional cocktail. Nothing to measure.

  1. Into the shaker

    Open the tin and pour the whole thing into a cocktail shaker over ice.

  2. Shake it hard

    A proper shake, like any cocktail. It chills the drink and brings the foam up.

  3. Strain and serve

    Strain into a chilled glass, add the garnish and it's ready. About a minute, start to finish.

Tinnies 101

What's in the tin?

A full cocktail, made by hand in Weymouth and sealed in a single serve tin. Spirit, liqueur, juice and syrup are all in there. It goes into the shaker as it is and you add the ice and the garnish.

How much is one tin?

One tin is one cocktail. Shaken and strained, it fills a coupe. No splitting, no measuring.

How long does it keep?

Sealed, it keeps in the fridge for months. The best before date is printed on every tin. Once it's open, serve it.

Do we need a cocktail bar?

No. A fridge, ice, a shaker and a glass. That's why cafés, pubs without a cocktail list, and event bars use it.

About Tipsy Tinnies

Tipsy Tinnies started in Weymouth in 2024 with one problem. A good cocktail takes a trained bartender, a stocked back bar and time the venue doesn't have on a Saturday night. So most places don't bother, and the guest who wanted a Pornstar Martini ends up with a gin and tonic.

We make the cocktail first, properly and by hand, then put it in a tin. Your staff tip it into a shaker, shake it, strain it, garnish it, exactly as they would any professional cocktail. It tastes the same at 6pm and at midnight, whoever's on the bar. We also run classes, pop up bars and staff training in person.

For venues

Is there a minimum order?

Trade orders are by the case, mixed flavours welcome. Tell us roughly what you get through in a week and we'll suggest a starting order.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across Dorset ourselves and ship further afield by courier. Get in touch with your postcode and we'll confirm.

Can we try it before we stock it?

Yes. We'll send a sample set, or come and shake the range for your team in person if you're nearby.

Do you do events outside Dorset?

Masterclasses and pop up bars travel. Distance affects the price, so give us the date and the postcode and we'll quote.

Can we sell the tins to take away?

Yes. Farm shops, delis and off licences stock them for the fridge. Same case pricing as venues.
